SINCE LAST SEASON

What changed from the old training list

The 2023/24 list · a PDF

  • Updated by hand: "Updated 14 February 2024", emailed around, versions everywhere
  • Most cohorts marked TBA, with no way to know when that changes
  • "Register your interest": an email to the secretariat, no seat counts
  • No record of what happened: who attended, who was certified, what it proved

BizFund2 PULSE · alive

  • One living schedule, current the moment anything changes, with a visible change feed
  • TBA becomes a status that moves: Planned → Dates confirmed → Open → Running → Completed
  • One-tap interest per cohort, with live seat counts the secretariat can plan around
  • Every completed cohort keeps its proof: enrolled, attended, certified, feeding the dashboard above
HOW IT WORKS

Three steps from interest to credential

1

Browse

Filter the live schedule by sector, division and status. Every cohort shows its real dates and seats.

2

Register your interest

One tap on the cohort you want. The secretariat confirms your seat and sends the details.

3

Attend and get certified

Your attendance is recorded properly at every session, and your certificate goes on record for employers and banks.

LIVE TRANSPARENCY

One live dashboard the whole state can read

Providers key in each session’s attendance, and everyone reads the same picture: locality coverage, trainee numbers and certificates, per division and per programme, refreshed after every class.

WHO GETS WHAT

Three stakeholders, one shared picture

Client · SBF

Answers without asking

  • Live view of every division, programme and ringgit
  • Committee papers prepared from real data, not memory
  • A defensible record for every decision and payment
Executor · Training providers

Less admin, faster money

  • Apply once, track status, no chasing the secretariat
  • Key in attendance once; it becomes report and claim evidence
  • Payment on a visible 14-day clock after verification
Learners · Trainees

Training that shows up on paper

  • Simple registration through their chosen provider
  • Attendance recorded properly, so completion counts
  • A certificate on record they can show an employer or bank

Behind the portal: the BizFund2 Programme Management Office

The portal does not run the programme; people do. The appointed PMO secretariat screens providers, publishes schedules, visits training sites, verifies evidence and prepares committee papers, so every number on this page stays honest. The dashboard is simply where that work becomes public.
