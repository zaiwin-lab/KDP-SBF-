THE REAL PROBLEMS

Three points where funding programmes usually break

We are not digitising everything. We are fixing the three bottlenecks that cost SBF time, trust and money, and leaving the rest to people who do it well.

PROBLEM 1

Proposals arrive as paper and email

Provider applications sit in inboxes, attachments go missing, and nobody can say where a proposal stands or why it stalled.

Digital fix: one application form, one pipeline, visible status for every proposal.
PROBLEM 2

Nobody sees training as it happens

Attendance lives in photocopied forms. Numbers arrive months late, so SBF reports old news and problems surface after the money is spent.

Digital fix: one dashboard for locality, trainee numbers and certificates, updated per session.
PROBLEM 3

Providers wait too long to be paid

Claims bounce between offices for missing evidence. Good trainers stop applying because payment feels like a lottery.

Digital fix: claims with evidence attached, a tracked verification flow, payment inside 14 days.

This is a hybrid PMO. Committee decisions, site visits, coaching and relationships stay human, run by KOBIS Berhad together with the SBF secretariat. The platform only carries the three flows above, which is why it is simple enough to use from day one.

STAGE 1 · APPLICATIONS

Provider proposals, out of the inbox and into a pipeline

Every training provider applies through the same online form, attaching their programme outline, costing and track record. From that moment the proposal has a number, a stage and a visible status, for the provider and for the secretariat.

  • One form, one standard. Programme outline, target learners, locality, costing and provider credentials.
  • Screening by the PMO. Completeness and eligibility checked within 5 working days.
  • Panel evaluation stays human. The SBF panel decides; the platform just gives them tidy papers and records the outcome.
  • Every applicant gets an answer. Approved, revise and resubmit, or declined with a reason.

Commitment: verified claims paid within 14 days.

When a programme's sessions are complete in the dashboard, the claim almost writes itself: attendance, certificates and photos are already attached as evidence. The provider submits, then watches the same four steps the PMO watches.

  • Evidence travels with the claim. No more chasing attendance sheets after the fact.
  • One clock, visible to all. Every claim shows its age; nothing sits quietly in a drawer.
  • SBF keeps the final say. Approval stays with the secretariat and committee; the platform just makes it fast.
