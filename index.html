PMO / 01

KOBIS Berhad as the Programme Management Office

“Sparkling Sarawak’s Future, Toward 2030 and Beyond.”

BizFund2 will move public money through dozens of providers to thousands of entrepreneurs. That demands more than administration: it demands an office that plans, verifies, reports and answers for every ringgit and every session. KOBIS Berhad proposes to be that office.

Our commitment to SBF rests on four disciplines, and every screen in this prototype exists to prove one of them.

Structured programme management

One lifecycle, fourteen controlled stages, applied identically to every programme in the portfolio.

Transparent governance

Every decision traceable along the chain from Premier Office to participant, with nothing moving off the record.

Data-driven execution

Budgets, attendance, claims and outcomes captured once, verified, and visible to stakeholders in real time.

Stakeholder collaboration

Secretariat, ministries, providers and entrepreneurs working inside one system instead of around it.
