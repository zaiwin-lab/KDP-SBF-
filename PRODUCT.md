# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Executive evaluators deciding who runs the BizFund2 Programme Management Office: Sarawak Business Federation (SBF) committee members, MINTRED officials, and Premier Office stakeholders. They review this on a laptop in a boardroom or on a tablet between meetings. Their job: judge in minutes whether KOBIS Berhad can be trusted with governance, money flow, and delivery for a state-wide entrepreneur funding programme. Secondary viewers: training providers and entrepreneurs who appear in the demo as personas.

## Product Purpose

A winning demo website (digital PMO prototype) for KOBIS Berhad's proposal to run the BizFund2 PMO for SBF. It transforms a slide-deck proposal (governance framework, programme lifecycle, PMO responsibilities, monitoring and reporting, core team) plus 7 value-added digital services (platform, executive dashboard, provider portal, entrepreneur portal, media management, analytics, future AI) into a flagship GovTech experience. Success = SBF says yes: the prototype must make the digital PMO feel already real, operational, and 5-10 years ahead.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, forward-looking. Corporate PMO gravitas with GovTech ambition: "Sparkling Sarawak's Future, Toward 2030 and Beyond." Feels like a flagship national digital platform (think state-level command centre), not a brochure. Data-driven confidence: dashboards, KPIs, live-feeling numbers. Respectful of government hierarchy (Premier Office → MINTRED → SBF → PMO) while clearly modern.

## Anti-references

- Typical Malaysian government portal: cluttered, dated, banner-heavy, comic-sans-adjacent.
- Generic SaaS landing page: cream background, gradient blobs, hero-metric template, identical icon-card grids.
- A slide deck converted to scrolling sections with no interactivity.
- Anything that feels like a cheap template rather than a bespoke national programme platform.

## Design Principles

1. Show the platform, don't describe it: render believable dashboard, portal, and analytics interfaces with realistic Sarawak data (districts, programmes, budgets).
2. Governance is the hero: hierarchy, flow, and accountability rendered as clear visual structure (flows, timelines, org chains) rather than bullet lists.
3. Executive pace: every section answers "can they run this?" within one screen; skimmable in 3 minutes, deep on demand.
4. State-grade polish: motion, typography, and colour discipline of a national command centre; nothing decorative without meaning.
5. Preserve the proposal's business logic exactly (11 proposal sections + 7 value additions); redesign the experience, never the substance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast throughout; semantic HTML; keyboard-reachable interactive demos; prefers-reduced-motion alternatives for all animation; 4-language support (EN / BM / ZH / IB) per KOBIS standard build.
